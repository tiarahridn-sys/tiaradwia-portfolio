Recruitment Case Study

AI-assisted sourcing workflow with human-led recruitment judgment.

A structured portfolio case study showing how AI can support role analysis, keyword planning, Boolean search preparation, candidate comparison notes, and profile validation without replacing recruiter responsibility.

01

Problem

Early-stage recruitment work often needs quick role understanding, clean search keywords, and organized screening notes. Without a structure, sourcing can become scattered and difficult to explain.

02

Goal

Build a repeatable workflow that helps translate job requirements into search terms, screening criteria, candidate notes, and follow-up priorities.

03

Role

I positioned myself as recruitment support: preparing research structure, organizing criteria, documenting candidate signals, and keeping the review process clear for human decision-making.

Workflow

From role requirements to structured screening notes.

Step 1

Role Breakdown

Identify core responsibilities, must-have skills, nice-to-have skills, industry context, and possible job title variations.

Step 2

Keyword Mapping

Group keywords into job titles, tools, industries, seniority level, location terms, and exclusion terms when needed.

Step 3

Boolean Search Planning

Prepare search strings that can be adjusted for LinkedIn, job boards, databases, or manual candidate research.

Step 4

Candidate Comparison Notes

Use a consistent format to compare candidate profiles based on relevant experience, skills, role fit, and missing information.

Step 5

Human Review

Keep final screening judgment with the recruiter or hiring team, especially for context, communication quality, motivation, and fairness.

Output

Reusable recruitment support structure.

The output is a practical sourcing framework that can be reused for junior recruitment support tasks: role intake notes, keyword groups, Boolean search drafts, candidate comparison notes, and follow-up questions.

This approach makes AI useful as a thinking and organization tool while keeping candidate evaluation careful, contextual, and human-led.

Ethical Note

AI supports recruitment work, but it should not replace human judgment.
