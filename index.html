About Me

Admin discipline shaped into practical HR, recruitment, and operations support.

I'm an HR & Recruitment Virtual Assistant specialising in talent sourcing, candidate screening, and operational support. My work sits at the intersection of admin discipline and modern tools — I use AI, Boolean search, and sourcing platforms to help teams hire smarter, stay organised, and streamline their daily workflows.

My freelance recruiting experience is real: I've sourced, screened, and submitted candidates across multiple roles, with several progressing to hire. I document my sourcing process, build structured screening workflows, and share what I learn through LinkedIn content and portfolio case studies.

Outside recruitment, I bring admin discipline and operational clarity from e-commerce and learning program work — which means I also understand how hiring connects to broader team and business needs.
