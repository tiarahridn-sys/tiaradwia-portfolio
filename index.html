Sourcing Workflow

My process helps move from a job brief to a more useful shortlist.

1

Understand the Role

I review responsibilities, must-have skills, preferred background, soft skills, and possible growth indicators before starting the search.

2

Build the Search

I use Boolean logic, related keywords, AI-assisted keyword ideas, and sourcing tools to create a wider but more relevant candidate pool.

3

Screen the Fit

I review each profile against the role criteria, separate strong matches from potential matches, and document the reason behind each recommendation.

4

Prepare the Shortlist

I use AI as support, but final screening is based on manual validation, context, and practical judgment before candidates are recommended.
