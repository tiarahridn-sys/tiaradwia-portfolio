AI-Assisted Sourcing Workflow

My process turns a job brief into a cleaner shortlist.

1

Calibrate the Role

I study the job description, required skills, soft-skill expectations, culture fit, and growth potential instead of relying only on keywords.

2

Search with Logic

I use Boolean search, AI-supported keyword ideas, and sourcing tools to surface more relevant candidates while reducing noise.

3

Score and Validate

I use a 1-10 scoring framework to classify perfect matches, strong potentials, and candidates who should be filtered out.

4

Shortlist with Judgment

AI supports the process, but I manually verify profile fit before recommending candidates for the next stage.
