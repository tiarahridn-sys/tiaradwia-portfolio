The 5-Point High-Impact CV Framework. 5 Pilar Utama Penyusunan CV Berdaya Jual Tinggi.

Point 1

Harvard Standard & 6-Second Scan Standar Harvard & Pemindaian 6 Detik

Converting old CV formats into clean 1-column Harvard International layout — 100% ATS-friendly, error-free formatting designed to pass the 6-second recruiter glance. Mengubah format CV lama menjadi standar internasional Harvard (1 kolom, ATS-Friendly, bebas grafik error) yang terbukti memikat mata rekruter dalam 6 detik pertama.

Point 2

Google XYZ Bullet Framework Formula Dampak Google XYZ

Rewriting passive duty lists into measurable achievement bullets using Google's XYZ formula: "Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]". Menulis ulang deskripsi kerja pasif menggunakan Google XYZ Framework: mengubah kalimat pasif menjadi pencapaian terukur berbobot angka & persentase.

Point 3

ATS Keyword Mining Penambangan Kata Kunci ATS

Extracting exact industry keywords and hard/soft skills from target job descriptions so your CV passes automated ATS filter algorithms. Memasukkan kata kunci industri spesifik dan frasa keterampilan kunci agar CV lolos dari saringan Applicant Tracking System (ATS).

Point 4

Psychological Cover Letter Surat Lamaran Psikologis (Cover Letter)

Crafting a 3-paragraph psychological cover letter (Hook → Value Bridge → Call to Action) tailored to your target role, avoiding copy-paste templates. Menyusun surat lamaran 3 paragraf bernilai jual tinggi (Hook → Value Bridge → Call to Action) yang disesuaikan dengan posisi incaran, bukan template pasaran.

Point 5

Career Switcher Positioning Strategi Khusus Career Switcher

Bridging past non-linear experiences into valuable transferable skills, creating portfolio claims that recruiters respect and validate. Menghubungkan pengalaman masa lalu dengan keahlian baru (transferable skills) dan menyusun narasi portofolio yang diakui kompetensinya oleh rekruter.
