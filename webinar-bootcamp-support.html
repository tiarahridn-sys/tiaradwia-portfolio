Real Work & People Operations

Webinar and Bootcamp Support (MC & Virtual Event Coordinator)

Supported session flow, participant coordination, audience engagement, attendance recap, and class assistant responsibilities, with a facilitation approach focused on stronger participant satisfaction.

01

Challenge

Virtual events often face the challenge of maintaining audience attention and participation. The risk of a "snooze-fest" or lack of interaction can diminish learning effectiveness and attendee satisfaction.

02

Solution & Results

Through a dynamic facilitation approach and strategic use of interactive tools, we successfully achieved a 40% improvement in attendee satisfaction scores. This demonstrated that the audience was not only present but also actively engaged and highly satisfied with the experience provided.

03

Impact

This success directly contributed to the positive reputation of our learning events, enhanced audience retention, and strengthened the brand's image as a provider of engaging and high-quality content. This experience is highly relevant for a marketing coordinator role focused on engagement and customer experience.
