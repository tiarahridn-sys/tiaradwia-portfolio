HR Staff Training Portfolio (Quantum HR) Portofolio Pelatihan Staf HR (Quantum HR)

Documentation of independent practice and deliverables for HR Staff administration training, covering payroll preparation, legal drafting of company regulations (PP), employment agreements (PKWT/PKWTT), and operational Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs). Dokumentasi praktik mandiri dan deliverables pelatihan administrasi HR Staff, meliputi penyusunan payroll, legal drafting peraturan perusahaan (PP), perjanjian kerja (PKWT/PKWTT), serta penyusunan Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) operasional.

01

Payroll & Compensation Payroll & Kompensasi

Merancang spreadsheet perhitungan payroll karyawan bulanan lengkap dengan absensi, lembur, insentif, pemotongan, perhitungan PPh 21, dan iuran BPJS Ketenagakerjaan/Kesehatan.

Core Modules Modul Utama

Practical HR operational competency structure mastered. Struktur kompetensi praktis operasional HR yang dikuasai.

Modul 1

Payroll Administration Administrasi Payroll

Understanding payroll structure, basic salary, fixed/variable allowances, overtime, PPh 21, social security calculations (BPJS Health & Employment), and pay slips. Memahami struktur penggajian, upah pokok, tunjangan tetap/tidak tetap, lembur (overtime), PPh 21, kalkulasi jaminan sosial (BPJS Kesehatan & Ketenagakerjaan), dan slip gaji.

Modul 2

Hubungan Industrial Hubungan Industrial

Drafting articles on rights and obligations of employees/employers, working hours provisions, rest periods, employee discipline procedures, and warning letter sanctions in Company Regulations. Menyusun pasal-pasal hak dan kewajiban pekerja/pengusaha, ketentuan waktu kerja, jam istirahat, prosedur disiplin karyawan, serta sanksi surat peringatan di Peraturan Perusahaan.

Modul 3

Employment Agreement Perjanjian Kerja

Drafting PKWT (fixed-term agreement) and PKWTT (indefinite-term agreement) with probation clauses, termination rights, compensation, and valid legal protection. Membuat draf PKWT (pekerja waktu tertentu) dan PKWTT (pekerja waktu tidak tertentu) dengan klausul masa percobaan, hak PHK, kompensasi, dan perlindungan hukum yang sah.

Modul 4

Standard Operating Procedures Standard Operating Procedures

Drafting structured employee resignation SOP (asset handover, exit interview, work reference letter) and medical certificate SOP for accurate attendance records. Menyusun SOP pengunduran diri karyawan secara terstruktur (handover aset, exit interview, surat keterangan kerja) dan SOP surat keterangan sakit agar rekaman absensi akurat.

Modul 5

Competency Certification Sertifikasi Kompetensi

Completing all Quantum HR Staff Training evaluation modules with valid program completion as a sign of operational expertise. Menyelesaikan seluruh modul evaluasi pelatihan kompetensi Quantum HR Staff Training dengan kelulusan program yang valid sebagai tanda keahlian operasional.
